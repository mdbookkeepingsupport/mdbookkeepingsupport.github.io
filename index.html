About

The friendly neighbourhood bookkeeper.

I'm Maann — a Leicester-based, first-class accounting graduate who kept noticing the same thing. Most small business owners weren't bad with money. Their records were just a mess.

I started MD Bookkeeping Support to help local sole traders — barbers, hairdressers, nail techs, cleaners, valets, and café owners — get their books sorted without the stress.

You don't need a big firm. You need someone who understands your world, keeps your records clean, and hands everything to your accountant ready to go.
